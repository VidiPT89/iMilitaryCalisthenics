# Privacy Policy

Military Calisthenics is a fully offline app. This is the entire policy:

- **All data stays on your device.** Your profile (weight, height, age, sex, fitness level, goal, equipment), your weight history and your generated training plans are stored locally on your iPhone/iPad using SwiftData. None of it is uploaded, synced or transmitted anywhere.
- **No accounts, no login.** The app doesn't ask who you are beyond the numbers it needs to build your plan.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.** Nothing about your usage is collected or shared with anyone, including the developer.
- **No network access at all.** The app doesn't make any network requests.
- **Notifications** (if you enable workout reminders) are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device through Apple's local notification system — no server is involved.

If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it. There is nothing to request, export or ask to have removed from a server, because nothing ever leaves your device.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/VidiPT89/iMilitaryCalisthenics](https://github.com/VidiPT89/iMilitaryCalisthenics/issues).
